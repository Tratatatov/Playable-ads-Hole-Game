How to Use with AI (Vision Mode)
1
Take a screenshot of your UI mockup
Works with Figma exports, hand-drawn sketches, PSD screenshots, or any image of your UI design.
2
Copy the AI prompt below
Paste it along with your image into ChatGPT-4o, Claude 3.5, Gemini 1.5 Pro, or any Vision LLM.
3
Paste the AI response into Builder
Copy the JSON the AI returns, go to the Builder tab, paste it and click "Build in Cocos".
